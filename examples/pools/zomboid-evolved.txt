#   - Item IDs must be UNIQUE across the whole file. A duplicated ID rolls once

#   - Lines beginning with # and blank lines are ignored.
#   - Non-numeric characters in the weight field are stripped; a missing or
#     zero weight is treated as 1.
#

#   AmmoLoot 0.15). Archery (RadArchery) and black powder (Gunsmithing) are the
#   intended ranged path, so they are the common ranged loot here. Modern firearms
#   and their ammo are NOT in this pool (stripped 2026-09-26; also removed from world loot).
#   Dino bait summons that species for hunting:
#   herbivores are uncommon, predators rare, and T-Rex bait ultra rare.
#   Every ID was checked against the B42 + mod scripts installed on this server.
