description=[list][*]World map: the in-game world map and minimap show the painted Zomboid Evolved map (the same map as the live map at zomboid.wubcord.app) with the game's own street and place names on top. Printed and stash maps are unchanged; areas outside the painted map fall back to the vanilla terrain image.[*]Safe zone banner: crossing the sandbag ring into or out of a camp's shrinking safe zone slides in a banner (Aegis style) with the zone's rules and the Front Lines retreat timer, and says so when the server refuses building or destroying there. Needs Aegis.[*]Checkpoint lamps: lights the lamp guns on the Front Lines and at the camp checkpoints (steady lamplight, nothing to take), with the shot and muzzle flash when one fires.[*]Server browser branding: the Zomboid Evolved badge and a painted-map banner for the server in Favorites and the public list (only while the mod is enabled in the main-menu Mods list).[*]Every part can be switched off in Options > Mods > Zomboid Evolved Core.[/list]
